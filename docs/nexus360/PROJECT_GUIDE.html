How MARK IV is structured

MARK IV is the internal master workspace for the interior-solutions business. Nexus 360 is the CRM module inside it; the finance and project tools are peer business modules connected through shared records.

MARK IV · master workspaceHome dashboard, navigation, staff access and company-wide context.
Nexus 360 · CRMPre-closure Leads, Opportunities, client Accounts, Contacts, notes, activity and quote revisions.
Finance · accounting workflowCompany ledger, project ledger, cash diary, vendors, bills, invoices and margin reporting. Phase 2 foundation.
Projects · delivery workflowOne job per project, design decisions and approvals, milestones, purchasing, costs, completion. Phase 3 foundation.
Shared record layerStable IDs and explicit links preserve traceability between customer, job, files, decisions, income and costs.
Google Sheet · staff interface and sync targetA usable operational sheet with two-way business-record sync is a later Phase 4 goal; current connectivity checks are not full sync.

How CRM records connect

The relationship follows the lifetime of the customer. A Lead is a potential customer before closure; it should not own every future job or financial record forever.

RecordMeaningConnections
LeadPerson or household being qualified before the first confirmed job.Contact details, source, notes, reminders, files and pre-closure Opportunity.
OpportunityOne specific potential interior project / job being discussed and quoted.Source Lead, quote revisions, contacts, files, status, expected value; after Won it links to Account and can proceed to a Project.
AccountContinuing customer or household record after a confirmed win.Multiple Contacts, multiple current and past Opportunities, and multiple simultaneous Projects.
ProjectOne distinct delivery job, even when the customer has other jobs running.Account, originating Opportunity, designs/approvals, milestones, costs, invoices and project ledger.
Finance recordIncome, receipt, expense, bill or invoice with its own accounting meaning.Company ledger plus relevant Account/Project/Opportunity links where applicable; no conflating separate jobs.
Conversion rule: qualified prospects can have an Opportunity while remaining Leads. Only after the client confirms and the Opportunity is marked Won should the Lead convert into a continuing Account and Contact. A Lost opportunity must not create an Account. Existing clients may have new Opportunities without creating duplicate Accounts.

How the business workflow should flow

  1. Enquiry: create a Lead, record the source, contact details, requirements, notes, next action and relevant files.
  2. Qualification: create an Opportunity for one potential job. Keep it connected to the Lead while details and quotation versions are negotiated.
  3. Quote: maintain separate quote revisions and internal approval state. Client acceptance must not be confused with internal manager approval.
  4. Win or loss: record the outcome, date, responsible user and a loss reason when lost. Convert a won prospect to an Account and Contact once.
  5. Delivery: the confirmed job becomes its own Project. Capture design drafts, client-approved design, decisions, milestones and responsibility.
  6. Money trail: attach invoices, payments, vendor bills and expenses to the company ledger and the right project/account. Compare billed value and actual cost for realized margin.
  7. Review: dashboards summarize source records; they should not replace the traceable invoice, receipt, quote revision or expense entries underneath.

The connected record structure is being built in Phase 1B; finance, project delivery and full two-way Sheet sync remain later roadmap phases.

What is not complete or included yet?

  • Phase 1B health score is intentionally pending; it will be based on end-to-end behavior, access, data correctness, desktop/mobile acceptance, regression and staging release.
  • Google Sheets business-record two-way sync is Phase 4 and remains paused. No connectivity check should be presented as a completed round trip.
  • Full accounting ledgers, daily expense diary and margin/P&L reports belong to Finance Phase 2.
  • Full design approval lifecycle and job execution tools belong to Projects Phase 3.
  • Broad AI analysis and Odoo integration are not V1 requirements.
  • GST/tax workflows are excluded for now. This does not replace the owner’s CA advice or create tax documents.
  • The local tracker and this guide refresh only when their HTML files are updated and the user refreshes the browser; they are not currently live dashboards backed by a real-time event feed.
